Recovery

From OAI CU/DU Lab

Safe order

  1. Stop the selected scenario through the operator console.
  2. Confirm remaining core and softmodem processes.
  3. Remove only scenario-owned firewall, route, tunnel, and MTU changes.
  4. Preserve management access.
  5. Connect either available B210 to MiniPC, then confirm live UHD discovery on USB3.
  6. Restore the MiniPC Ethernet profile.
  7. Validate machine gates, then phone gates.

Do not delete blindly

Host source trees, local configuration, and evidence may be required for rollback. Establish ownership and map the exact dependency before removing anything.

Warning: a printed stop message is not proof of clean state. Check processes, routes, tunnels, firewall rules, MTU, and radio ownership.
