Quectel boundary

The maintained path uses the monolithic Firecell donor gNB only to serve the Quectel modem; that donor has no F1 association. The selected access DU is the only DU in this workflow, and its F1-C and F1-U must use wg-quectel-f1, with WireGuard outer traffic on the detected Quectel data interface.

The modem must camp only on the independent donor cell. Using the access cell as its own backhaul creates a circular dependency and is unsupported.

Transport isolation

During validation, verify that F1 traffic does not leak onto an unselected management, Ethernet, Wi-Fi, GRE, or WireGuard path.
