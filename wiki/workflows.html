Quectel boundary

A valid result requires donor-DU and access-DU F1 associations to the same CU. The modem must camp only on the independent donor cell. Access-DU F1-C and F1-U must use wg-quectel-f1, with WireGuard outer traffic on the detected modem interface.

Transport isolation

During validation, verify that F1 traffic does not leak onto an unselected management, Ethernet, Wi-Fi, GRE, or WireGuard path.
